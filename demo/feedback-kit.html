feedback-kit

Tap Send feedback: the panel shows a picture of the chart as it is now, to draw on, and links to a new issue filled in with what was on screen. The module comes from ../feedback-kit/, the branch this page was served from.

What to look at

  • The picture keeps the title's web font (loading…), the colours from CSS variables and classes, and the gradient whose colours are set inside the SVG.
  • Draw with a finger, the Pencil or the mouse; Undo (or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) and Clear.
  • Try it in light and dark mode, and upright and sideways on the iPad.

The issue

It opens in bremondr/web-kit with the demo label. It is a real issue: submit it only if you mean to, and close it afterwards.
